Research Experience

A Physics-Based Compact Model of the Single-Barrier ECC-pMTJ for Energy-Efficient In-Memory Computing

Preparing journal submission
  • Developed a Verilog-A compact model of a single-barrier ECC-pMTJ from coupled two-macrospin LLG dynamics, giving a closed-form critical current with no iterative solver.
  • Identified a retention ceiling intrinsic to single-free-layer MTJs (Δ = 44.9). The composite clears it by 1.19×, with 21.9% less switching current in a 1.53× smaller junction at iso-retention.
  • Validated sequential STT reversal in Cadence Virtuoso on GPDK 90 nm at D = 17 nm, reaching Δ = 44.0 and Ic0 = 9.47 µA at ±0.8 V.
  • Benchmarked in NeuroSim+ on MNIST: 20.5% lower read latency, 9.5% lower total latency and 8.5% lower write energy at comparable accuracy.
In-Memory ComputingMTJVerilog-ANeuroSim+

Evaluating Critical-Path Recovery from Pre-Routing Timing GNNs

Submitted to ISQED 2027
  • Built a label-consistent benchmark of 55,977 OpenSTA critical paths over 21 designs of the TimingPredict dataset (SkyWater 130 nm). Using the dataset authors' STA build with CRPR disabled alone changes the critical path on about 23% of endpoints.
  • Measured how often STA's critical path can be recovered from TimingGCN and PreRoutGNN predictions with a unateness-aware backtracking rule (95.5% from exact timing): 40.7% and 55.8% on 21,958 held-out endpoints, against 49.9% for pre-routing STA that ignores wires.
  • Showed that recovery collapses on long paths and at close decisions, and that arrival-time R² does not predict it. Also tested attribution and faithfulness with gradient saliency and feature masking.

HybMem-SNN: Event-Driven SNN Accelerator

  • Designed an event-driven spiking neural network accelerator in Verilog (196-64-10 LIF neurons, 8-bit weights in block RAM) for MNIST, trained with surrogate gradients in Python. It reaches 96.96% test accuracy and skips 82.7% of weight-row reads by processing only spiking inputs.
  • Added a hybrid reliable/unreliable weight-memory model with a bit-error injector in RTL. Errors in the four least significant weight bits cause almost no accuracy loss. The RTL matches a Python cycle model exactly in spike counts and cycles in Vivado simulation.
  • Synthesised and routed on a Zynq-7020 with Vivado: 7,948 LUTs, 5.5 BRAM tiles, 0 DSPs, timing met at 100 MHz (119.6 µs per image).
